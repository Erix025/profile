I am a first-year PhD student at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/), supervised by [Prof. Bohan Zhuang](https://bohanzhuang.github.io/), and a Research Intern at [Qwen](https://qwen.ai/). My research focuses on efficient long-context AI through algorithm–system co-design, with an emphasis on sparse and linear attention and KV cache compression for language and multimodal generation.

Previously, during my bachelor's studies at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/), I earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Technology and led [ZJUSCT](https://www.zjusct.io/), the university's supercomputing team, which has won several international supercomputing competitions.
